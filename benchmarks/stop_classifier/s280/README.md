## What prompted the session

The Stop hook's proceed arm blocks a turn from ending and injects the
classifier's `reason` into the agent **verbatim, as its next instruction**. Nobody
had counted how often that instruction was wrong. Measured over 47 days of real
transcripts: **117 fires, ~57% defective.**

## Measured results (294 live calls, `gpt-oss:20b` on MS-S1)

Same model, same 49-case corpus, production transport, **only the system prompt varied.**

| arm | delivered | correct/49 | proceed | pause | dispatch | unsafe | p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FULL (shipped) | 39 (80%) | 16 (33%) | 0/18 | 16/16 | 0/5 | 0 | 39.9 s |
| SLIM | 43 (88%) | 21 (43%) | 5/21 | 15/21 | 1/1 | **6** | 41.3 s |
| SLIM2 | 45 (92%) | 32 (65%) | 11/22 | 21/22 | 0/1 | 1 | 23.9 s |
| SLIM3 | 49 (100%) | 40 (82%) | **18/22** | 20/22 | 2/5 | 2 | 18.1 s |
| SLIM4 | 47 (96%) | 38 (78%) | 13/20 | 21/22 | 4/5 | 1 | 25.0 s |
| **SLIM5** | **49 (100%)** | **42 (86%)** | 15/22 | **22/22** | **5/5** | **0** | **16.2 s** |

An always-pause bot scores **22/49 (44.9%)** on this corpus. **The shipped prompt
scores below that floor.**

⚠️ These are **single-pass** figures with no variance estimate, and SLIM through
SLIM5 were tuned against these same 49 cases — the 42/49 is in-sample. PLAN-0122
§9 states the limitations in full; do not quote these numbers without them.

## What is deliberately absent

- **No rubric scores.** See above.
- **No held-out numbers.** `gold.yaml`'s 30 cases could not be run until D-1 was
  fixed — all 30 carry their expected label in the transcript filename. That is
  PLAN-0122 Step 3.
- **No qwen arms.** Both `qwen3.8:27b` candidates measured 54–68 s per call,
  inside the 45–120 s band `../RESULTS.md` disqualified a priori for a hook that
  fires at every turn end. The problem a model swap was meant to solve was fixed
  by prompt wording on the incumbent.
